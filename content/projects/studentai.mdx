## The Challenge & Objective

Student attrition poses significant institutional challenges in higher education, impacting both academic completion rates and university resource allocation. The objective of this project was to explore the full data science lifecycle to detect early behavioral and demographic warning indicators associated with academic dropouts.

Key goals included:
* **Data Cleaning & Standardization:** Transforming raw, noisy academic CSV records into curated, normalized datasets ready for statistical modeling.
* **Comparative Algorithm Benchmarking:** Evaluating multiple supervised classification models under standardized train/test splits.
* **Feature Interpretability:** Identifying which academic metrics (credits completed, attendance, grade trajectories) have the highest predictive weight in student retention.

## Data & Machine Learning Pipeline

```
  [Raw Academic Records (CSV)]
                │
                ▼
  1. Data Preprocessing & Cleaning (Handling missing values, outlier pruning)
                │
                ▼
  2. Exploratory Data Analysis (Correlation matrices & persona profiling)
                │
                ▼
  3. Feature Engineering & Scaling (Train/Test splits, categorical encoding)
                │
                ▼
  4. Model Training & Evaluation (Benchmarking Logistic Regression, Trees, KNN, Boosting)
                │
                ▼
  [Retention Risk Assessment & Performance Metrics]
```

1. **Data Preprocessing:** Implemented structured cleaning pipelines (`cleaning_and_preprocessing.ipynb`) to address inconsistencies, impute missing entries, and remove anomalies.
2. **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):** Created focused persona analysis notebooks (`Analysis_Persona2.ipynb`) to uncover latent trends across student cohorts and academic milestones.
3. **Model Selection & Training:** Built standalone model scripts (`0_logistic_regression.py`, `1_decision_tree.py`, `2_Gradient_Boosting.py`, `2_KNN_model.py`) to systematically train and benchmark predictive performance.

---

## Key Technical Highlights

* **Multi-Model Evaluation:** Compared linear baselines against non-linear ensemble models to evaluate trade-offs between interpretability and predictive accuracy.
* **Structured Data Pipeline:** Enforced reproducible preprocessing steps ensuring data transformations applied to training partitions carried over identically to testing datasets without data leakage.
* **Persona-Based Analysis:** Clustered students into risk profiles to provide actionable qualitative insights alongside quantitative classification metrics.
* **Modular Codebase:** Organized preprocessing notebooks and model scripts into modular components with documented dependencies (`requirements.txt`).
