## The Challenge & Origin

Developed during **HackSpain 2026** for the **Prosper Track**, this project addresses one of the most expensive operational headaches in outpatient healthcare: phone-based clinic scheduling and sudden doctor cancellations.

In real-world clinics (such as the challenge's fictional *Clínica Arenal* in Madrid), scheduling is not a simple chat interaction—it is a strict multi-variable constraint problem involving:
* **Patient identity & insurance policies** (e.g., verifying whether specific slots are payable with Adeslas, Sanitas, or private coverage).
* **Provider specialties & multi-site coverage** (cross-referencing doctors, locations, and authorized languages).
* **Referral & triage requirements** (filtering emergency symptoms according to Manchester Triage protocols).

### The Core Thesis: *"The model speaks; the system decides."*

When relying solely on conversational voice models, agents tend to hallucinate convenient slot times or invent patient records just to maintain conversational flow. **ClinicReflow** pairs low-latency, voice-native conversational models with a strict deterministic Python backend that enforces clinical rules, verifies insurance IDs, and submits exact booking actions.

## Architecture & Audio Pipeline

```
  [Caller / Phone (Twilio Media Streams)]
                   │  (µ-law 8 kHz audio)
                   ▼
  [FastAPI WebSocket Gateway (/ws)]
                   │
                   ▼
  [Voice Engine: Gemini 3.8 Live via Pipecat]
                   │  (Native audio-to-audio)
                   ▼
  [Deterministic Brain (16 Clinic Tools)]
   ├── Date Resolution (Europe/Madrid in Python)
   ├── Insurance Validation (payable_with enums)
   ├── Real-Time Availability (Prosper API)
   └── Emergency Escalation (Manchester Triage → 112)
                   │
                   ▼
  [Prosper Platform Submission & Action Flush]
```

1. **Audio Streaming Gateway:** Ingests inbound calls over Twilio Media Streams via WebSockets (µ-law 8 kHz), converting audio chunks into native PCM frames without intermediate speech-to-text transcoding.
2. **Native Multimodal Voice:** Utilizes **Gemini 3.8 Live** (`gemini-3.8-live`) to manage real-time turn-taking and dynamic bilingual switching (Spanish/English) with sub-second voice latency.
3. **Deterministic Tool Execution:** The LLM delegates real-world actions to 16 specialized backend tools that interact directly with the Prosper Clinical API, ensuring zero slot hallucinations.
4. **Action Flush on Hangup:** Automatically dispatches validated booking, registration, rescheduling, or emergency escalation payloads to the platform within strict challenge time limits.

## Key Technical Highlights

* **Voice-Native vs. Cascaded Audio:** Bypassed fragile multi-hop cascades (STT $\to$ LLM $\to$ TTS) in favor of direct audio-to-audio streaming, eliminating latency spikes and awkward conversational interruptions.
* **Deterministic Date & Calendar Resolution:** Relative date expressions in natural language (*"el jueves que viene por la tarde"*) are resolved deterministically in Python against timezone-aware business calendars (`Europe/Madrid`), preventing date arithmetic hallucinations.
* **Rigorous Insurance Verification:** Verifies insurance plan compatibility (`payable_with`) on every candidate slot before confirming appointments to eliminate insurance rejection errors.
* **Manchester Triage Safety Protocol:** Recognizes high-risk clinical symptoms and triggers automated priority escalation to emergency services (112 / SAMUR) when necessary.
* **Unified Single-Port Deployment:** Orchestrated as a single-process Fly.io service running the WebSocket voice pipeline (`/ws`), health checks (`/healthz`), and a jury-facing real-time auditing console (`/ops`).
