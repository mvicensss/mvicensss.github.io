# Velaris: The City of Starlight

An immersive web experience designed to promote tourism in Velaris, the mythical City of Starlight inspired by the A Court of Thorns and Roses book series.

This project was developed as a class assignment in collaboration with a classmate, with focus on semantic HTML structure, style customization, and interactive libraries.

## The Challenge

The goal was to build an attractive and fully responsive one-page landing site that lets users explore a fictional tourist destination.

Navigation was handled through modal windows instead of separate pages to create a fluid and modern experience.

## Technologies and Tools

* **Structure and Styling:** HTML5, CSS3, SASS.
* **Interactivity:** JavaScript (Vanilla and jQuery).
* **External Libraries:**
  * Swiper.js for touch-enabled image galleries.
  * Particles.js for visual background effects.
* **Base UI:** Dimension template by HTML5 UP, deeply adapted to fit the project style.

## Key Features

* **Modal Navigation:** The main page acts as a central menu. Clicking sections like City, Culture, or Stay loads content in a modal with a depth effect.
* **Dynamic Galleries:** Auto-playing Swiper carousels were used to showcase city areas such as the Rainbow District and the Sidra River.
* **Thematic Design and Form:** The palette was adapted to navy, gold, and cream tones. The contact form includes thematic fields and validation.
* **Optimized Loading:** Images were preloaded in sliders to reduce visual jumps.

## My Role and Collaboration

In this two-person project, we divided tasks to keep a smooth workflow. My contributions included:

* **Visual Theming and Effects:** Theme adaptation and Particles.js setup for the starry background.
* **Content and HTML Structure:** Content writing plus semantic structures including lists and tables.
* **Interactive Media:** Swiper integration and gallery configuration across sections.
* **Form Development:** Customized form fields and required validation controls.
* **Testing and Deployment:** QA support for readability and final GitHub Pages deployment.
